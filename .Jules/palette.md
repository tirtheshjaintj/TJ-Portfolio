## 2025-05-18 - Floating Action Buttons as Interactive Elements
**Learning:** Floating action elements (like dark mode toggles or chat triggers) rendered as plain `div` tags with `onClick` lack keyboard navigability, focus rings, and screen reader labels. By converting them to `<button type="button">` with existing utility classes (`btn float d-flex align-items-center justify-content-center p-0 border-0`) along with `aria-label` and `title`, they immediately become accessible to keyboard and assistive technology users without introducing custom CSS or visual regressions.
**Action:** When inspecting floating widgets or icon-only controls, convert nested non-semantic `div`s to semantic `<button>` elements with `aria-label` and ensure inner icons are marked with `aria-hidden="true"`.
